The driver header names the cron tick as the reserver of the selected phase, and says that the one-phase `--session` mode starts no reservation and writes no claim. The header had claimed that the server reserves the selected phase for every mode, and a reader who believed it would plan a resume or a cleanup on a fence that `--session` never takes.
